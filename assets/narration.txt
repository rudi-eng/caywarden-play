June, seventeen eighteen. Woodes Rogers has sailed into Nassau with the King's Pardon... but ye kept the black flag. The whole Atlantic lies open: the Caribbees, the Spanish Main, the American colonies, the coasts of Europe and Guinea. Trade, plunder, buried gold... and the navy hunting ye all the while. Northeast of Nassau, Driftwood Cay awaits: your own island to build into a pirate haven. Every harbour factor buys and sells company shares dated from the spring of seventeen ten. Amsterdam, Bristol, Cadiz, La Rochelle and Lisbon deal cheaper, and a prize ye take moves those prices when the month turns.
